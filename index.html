The John Smythe method

Investigate. Verify. Compare. Decide.

John Smythe's job is not to make a political decision for the reader. The objective is to make the underlying evidence easier to find, understand and challenge.

01

Investigate

Identify the claim, decision, policy or event and establish exactly what needs to be examined.

02

Verify

Locate primary documents and authoritative records wherever possible and distinguish verified facts from allegations.

03

Compare

Put competing claims and positions alongside each other with relevant context and their original sources.

04

Decide

Readers inspect the evidence and reach their own conclusions. John Smythe does not choose a political party for them.

Public participation

The John Smythe Evidence Desk

Have we missed something? Challenge the analysis. Submit the source. Correct the record. Reader submissions are reviewed before they can become part of the John Smythe evidence archive.

What counts as useful evidence?

The strongest submissions allow the source to be independently checked.

  • Official documents and government publications
  • Legislation and parliamentary records
  • Court judgments and official decisions
  • Official statistics and published datasets
  • Original speeches, interviews and policy documents
  • Reliable contemporary reporting
  • Documents whose origin can be independently established

Unsupported allegations, fabricated documents and manipulated material are not evidence simply because they have been posted online.

Reader discussion

Challenge the Argument

Future investigations will allow moderated reader discussion. Disagreement is welcome. Evidence is even better.

Discussion should improve the investigation.

Readers will be able to question an interpretation, identify missing context, provide counter-evidence and discuss competing conclusions.

Reader comments will remain clearly separate from John Smythe's published material and evidence archive.

Discussion Standard

Challenge claims and ideas rather than attacking people because of race, nationality, ethnicity or religion.

Serious allegations require evidence. Material that appears fabricated, unlawful, threatening or deliberately misleading should not be published.

And yes — readers may challenge John Smythe itself.

About John Smythe

Independent. Evidence-Led. British.

Proud of Britain.
Independent of government.
Accountable to evidence.

John Smythe is an independent British online project focused on evidence, accountability, public awareness and preserving the record for future generations.

Governments, political parties and institutions change. The evidential standard should not change with them.

Political parties are compared using the same principles. John Smythe does not endorse a party or award political winners. Readers should be able to inspect the material and reach their own conclusions.

Criticism of governments, political movements, religions, ideologies and institutions should focus on ideas, policies, evidence and documented conduct — not collective hostility towards people because of their identity.

When John Smythe gets something materially wrong, the record should be corrected.
